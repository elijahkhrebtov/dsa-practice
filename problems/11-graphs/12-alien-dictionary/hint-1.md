# Hint 1

The list only tells you something where two **adjacent** words first differ. In `"wrt"` vs `"wrf"`, the first difference is `t` vs `f`, so `t` comes before `f` — and the letters after that position tell you nothing.

What does a pair like `"abc"` followed by `"ab"` tell you? What should happen when the second word is a prefix of the first?
