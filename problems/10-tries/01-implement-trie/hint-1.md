# Hint 1

Words like `apple`, `app` and `apply` all begin with `app`. What if those shared letters were stored only **once**, and each word continued from there along its own branch?

Picture a tree where every edge is one letter and every root-to-node path spells a prefix. What would `startsWith` have to do in that tree? And what extra piece of information would `search` need that `startsWith` doesn't?
