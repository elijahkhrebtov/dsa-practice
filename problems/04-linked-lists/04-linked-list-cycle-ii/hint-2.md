# Hint 2

Name the distances: `a` = steps from the head to the cycle start, `b` = steps from the cycle start to the meeting point, `L` = cycle length.

When they meet, slow has walked `a + b` steps and fast has walked twice that. Fast's extra `a + b` steps were whole laps of the loop, so `a + b` is a multiple of `L`. What does that tell you about walking `a` more steps from the meeting point?
