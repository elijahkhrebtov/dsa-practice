# Hint 1

Picture walking through the days from left to right. When a hot day arrives, which earlier days does it answer? All the earlier days that are colder and are *still waiting* for a warmer day.

Look at the days that are still waiting at any moment. If day `i` came before day `j` and both are still waiting, how must their temperatures compare? (If `i` were colder, day `j` itself would already have answered it.)
